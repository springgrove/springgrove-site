Springgrove is a Geneva-based B2B intermediary. We structure and execute transactions between authorized sources and qualified counterparties: brands, distributors, retailers and capital partners, across Europe, the Middle East, Asia Pacific and the Americas.

Scroll

An intermediary built for professional counterparties.

We source from authorized dealers, brand distributors and specialists, then place inventory with a curated network of trade clients. The model is asset-light: revenue is earned on transaction margin, with every deal documented end to end.

Organized under Swiss law and based in Geneva, Springgrove operates with the discretion the trade expects and the compliance its counterparties require.
